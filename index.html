Product initiative

Live match workflow optimization

How I approach a product problem: map the flow, find the delay, test the fix on live traffic.

Problem

Match updates lagged during high-traffic events because of manual dependencies and CMS bottlenecks.

Approach

Mapped the full publishing flow, found the delay points, worked with engineering to validate tool-level fixes, and tightened coordination during live events. Tested changes in live matches and tracked lag across events.

Outcome

Lower update lag, a more consistent real-time experience for users, and a reusable framework for future high-traffic tournaments.
